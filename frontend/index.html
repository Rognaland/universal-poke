💡 Help & Rules

🃏 Texas Hold'em Basics

Each player receives 2 private cards. Five community cards are revealed in stages (Flop, Turn, River). Make the best 5-card hand using any combination of your hole cards and the board.

Hand Rankings: Royal Flush → Straight Flush → Four of a Kind → Full House → Flush → Straight → Three of a Kind → Two Pair → Pair → High Card

💰 How Betting Works

  • Blinds: Small blind (SB) and big blind (BB) are forced bets that start each hand
  • Actions: Fold (quit hand), Check (pass), Call (match bet), Raise (increase bet)
  • All-In: Bet all remaining chips - can win main pot + side pots
  • Showdown: Remaining players reveal cards, best hand wins the pot

⛓️ Blockchain Integration

  • Token: WBSTR (Wrapped BSTR) on LUKSO mainnet - LSP7 standard
  • Conversion: 1 chip = 1,000 WBSTR (e.g., 100 chips = 100,000 WBSTR)
  • Automatic: Deposits, escrow, and payouts happen on-chain - fully transparent
  • Gas-Free: Server covers transaction costs - you only pay the buy-in

❓ FAQ

Q: What if I lose connection?
A: The server will auto-fold your hand after timeout. Your remaining stack stays in escrow.

Q: How long do payouts take?
A: Instant! Blockchain transactions process automatically when the game ends.

Q: Is there a house rake?
A: Yes, 3% fee taken on deposit. Example: Deposit 100 chips → 97 chips in play, 3 to house.
